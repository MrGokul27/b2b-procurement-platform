Our Evolution

Journey Timeline

From a boutique supplier consultancy to a global multi-enterprise B2B exchange network.

2014 Founding Stage

Platform Founded in London

Established Stackly as a regional software consultancy focused on simplifying supplier RFQ documentation for UK metal and industrial manufacturers.

2018 Cloud & Scale

Cloud Migration & API Release

Launched our fully hosted SaaS cloud platform. Released ready-made integration APIs for SAP ledgers, Oracle NetSuite, and Microsoft Dynamics databases.

2022 Global Expansion

Global Network Expansion

Expanded operating infrastructure to cover APAC and North American trade channels. Onboarded over 5,000 vetted international suppliers with automated compliance checks.

2026 AI Era

Automated Spend Analytics Suite

Integrated advanced predictive analytics tools to forecast budget leakages, automate supplier matching, and calculate real-time ESG logistics footprint scores.
